## Claims we can make, and ones we cannot

- Can: the statement and its password are read on your own device and never
  uploaded. Pasted messages are read to fill in the list and are not kept.
- Can: it checks the statement adds up and compares the result with the
  statement's balance.
- Cannot: "connects to M-Pesa", "syncs with M-Pesa" or "automatic". It is an
  import you choose to do.
- Cannot: promise the balance always matches exactly. Fuliza repayments and loans
  are not recorded as spending or income, so a difference is normal, and Jamvi
  shows what makes it up.
