## App name (30 characters max)

**Jamvi: M-Pesa budget** (20)

## Short description (80 max)

**Your M-Pesa, sorted: see where every shilling went, and budget the month ahead.** (79)

## Full description (4,000 max)

```
Jamvi fills in your budget from your M-Pesa. Who you paid, what it was for, what came in - without typing every entry.

START WITH ONE STATEMENT
Choose the day to start from, ask M-Pesa for your statement, and Jamvi reads it in: every payment, every deposit, Fuliza, airtime and bundles, sorted into categories you can check before anything is saved.

THEN JAMVI KEEPS UP
Allow it, and Jamvi reads M-Pesa's new messages each time you open it - only M-Pesa's own messages, and nothing is saved until you look through them. Or share messages to Jamvi from your Messages app.

SEE WHERE IT WENT
- Spending by category, month by month
- What came in, and from where
- A budget for each category, and how far through it you are
- Search any payment by name, amount or date

YOUR BUSINESSES, KEPT APART
Run a side business? Name it, say which payments are its sales and costs, and Jamvi keeps them out of your personal figures - with a profit report for each business.

PEOPLE AND DEBTS
Who owes you, who you owe, money lent and borrowed, chamas and merry-go-rounds.

SHARE A BUDGET
Invite family or a partner to a shared group. Groups cost nothing extra.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Jamvi never moves money and never asks for your M-Pesa PIN. It reads only M-Pesa's messages, only with your permission, and keeps only the entries you save.

Free for 14 days, then a monthly subscription. Made in Kenya by Optimum Prime Solutions.
```

Before submitting, check the trial length and price against the app (`jamvi-pricing-model`,
`TRIAL_DAYS`) and against the payment decision in [README.md](README.md): if Play Billing is
not used, the last line must not mention paying at all.
