---
name: Recurring expense budget handoff
description: Product rule for collecting a recurring expense's monthly planning amount.
---

Marking a new expense as recurring should take the user to the Budget tab and ask for the average monthly amount for that category. After the budget is saved or the prompt is cancelled, return to the originating expense form without losing its draft.

**Why:** The monthly figure is a budgeting decision, not merely another expense field, and users should understand where it affects their plan.

**How to apply:** Keep this handoff consistent across full expense entry, quick-log entry, and mobile. Preserve all unsaved expense fields during navigation and continue requiring a valid monthly amount before a recurring expense can be saved.
