---
name: All-round onboarding
description: Product rule for keeping Bajeti setup guidance complete across the workspace journey
---

Setup guidance should be all-round: help users establish the complete active workspace, including its budget, income, funding, goals, and relevant group participation.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the guide to help people finish a usable workspace rather than stopping after a single financial feature.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing onboarding, derive progress from live workspace data, include every required setup area, and keep workspace-specific steps such as member invitations conditional.
