---
name: All-round onboarding
description: Product rule for keeping Jamvi setup guidance complete across the workspace journey
---

Setup guidance should be all-round: help users establish the complete active workspace, including its budget, income, funding, goals, and relevant group participation. Onboarding is complete only after final approval; a saved draft or explicit incomplete record must override any existing-workspace fallback.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants the guide to help people finish a usable workspace rather than stopping after a single financial feature, and users who exit midway must return to their saved setup instead of landing on Home.

**How to apply:** Persist both answers and the current step, resume incomplete setup on the next visit, include every required setup area, and keep workspace-specific steps such as member invitations conditional.
