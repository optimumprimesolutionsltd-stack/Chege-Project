---
name: Manual category allocations
description: Product rule for entering the amount assigned to each expense category.
---

Every newly selected expense category must start with a blank amount. Never copy or synchronize the expense total into a category allocation, even when there is only one category. Once a category is selected, its amount is required and all category amounts must exactly total the expense before saving.

**Why:** A prefilled category amount can silently assign the wrong value and makes the editable field easy to overlook.

**How to apply:** Keep category allocation state independent from expense-total edits on quick web, full web, and mobile forms. Make the required amount field and exact-total status visually prominent.
