---
name: Shared transaction member rule
description: Eligibility policy for recording money in newly created shared workspaces.
---

New shared workspaces need two confirmed members before they can record expenses or contributions. This applies to regular, recurring-copy, single-goal, and cascading contribution actions.

**Why:** Shared money should not appear to be jointly agreed or tracked before another participant has actually joined the workspace.

**How to apply:** Keep My Budget usable for its single owner and grandfather legacy shared workspaces. Do not apply this restriction to group setup, invitations, categories, income sources, savings-goal setup, or bank activity. Surface the server-provided eligibility status in every client action that records shared money.
